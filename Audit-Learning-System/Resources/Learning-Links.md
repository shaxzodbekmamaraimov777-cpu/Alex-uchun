## 4. Official, credible places to earn certificates

> These are the globally recognised, exam-based credentials. Each link goes to the
> **official body** — register and book exams there, not through third parties.

| Certificate | Body | What it's for | Official link |

| **ACA** | ICAEW (UK) | Chartered accountant (audit-heavy, prestigious) | [icaew.com](https://www.icaew.com/learning-and-development/aca) |
| **CPA (US)** | AICPA | US Certified Public Accountant (audit licence in US) | [thiswaytocpa.com](https://www.thiswaytocpa.com/) · [aicpa-cima.com](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/) |
| **CIMA** | AICPA & CIMA | Chartered management accountant | [aicpa-cima.com/cima](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/cima-qualifications) |

## 5. Recommended order for *you* (HLB Tashkent intern)

| Phase | Earn this | Why now |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **During probation** | [MOS Excel Associate (MO-210)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/mos-excel-associate-m365-apps/) | Fast, exam-based, directly job-relevant. |
| **Start during probation** | [ACCA registration](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/student/getting-started.html) + first paper (FA or MA) | The career-defining qualification; your HLB job counts toward its experience requirement. |
| **Year 1–3** | ACCA Applied Skills (PM, FR, FM, **AA**) | Builds your core audit competence. |
| **Later / specialise** | [CIA](https://www.theiia.org/en/certifications/cia/) or [CISA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cisa) + [Power BI PL-300](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/data-analyst-associate/) | Audit specialisation + standout data skills. |

> **💡 Ask HLB:** many audit firms fund ACCA registration, exam fees, study materials and
> give paid study leave after probation. Ask HR — it's a common benefit.

## 6. Region note — Uzbekistan / Tashkent

- **ACCA in Tashkent:** ACCA is well established in Uzbekistan. Find local exam centres and
  approved tuition providers here → [ACCA exam centre / tuition finder](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/help/find.html). Also search: `ACCA exam centre Tashkent Uzbekistan`.
- **MOS Excel exam centres:** search [Certiport test centre locator](https://certiport.pearsonvue.com/Test-takers) or Google `Certiport testing center Tashkent`.
- **Local providers:** several Tashkent training centres offer ACCA classes and Excel
  courses with certificates — search `ACCA courses Tashkent` and `Excel kurslari Toshkent`.
- Confirm **local fees and currency** with the centre directly; international list prices differ.
