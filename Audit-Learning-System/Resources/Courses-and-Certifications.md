# Courses & Certifications — Get Officially Certified

> Where to learn each skill and how to earn **real, recognised certificates with exams**.
> Prices and details change — always confirm on the official site before paying.
> *Details below were checked for 2026; treat them as a guide, not a quote.*

---

## How to think about certifications

Two kinds of credentials matter for an auditor:

1. **The big professional qualification — ACCA** (or ACA/CPA). This is the career-defining
   one. Long-term, multi-year, globally respected. *This is the prize.*
2. **Skill certificates — Microsoft Office Specialist (Excel), data/analytics certs.**
   Fast to earn, prove a concrete tool skill, great for your CV and probation review.

**For your 2-month probation**, target a **quick win** (MOS Excel) and **start the ACCA
journey** (register + book your first paper). Quick wins prove momentum; ACCA proves
commitment.

## 1. ACCA Qualification (the main goal) ⭐

**What it is:** the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants qualification — the
globally recognised route to becoming a chartered certified accountant. It's what the FA,
MA, PM, FR, FM, AA files in this system teach.

**Structure (13 exams across 3 levels):**

| Level | Papers | Your files | Exam style |
|-------|--------|------------|------------|
| **Applied Knowledge** | BT, **MA**, **FA** | 01-FA, 02-MA | On-demand computer-based, 2 hrs |
| **Applied Skills** | LW, **PM**, TX, **FR**, **AA**, **FM** | 03-PM, 04-FR, 05-FM, 06-AA | Session-based (Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec), 3 hrs |
| **Strategic Professional** | SBL, SBR + 2 options (AFM/APM/ATX/AAA) | (future study) | Session-based, case-study |

Plus the **Ethics & Professional Skills module** and **3 years of practical experience
(PER)** — which your HLB job counts towards!

**Key facts:**
- **Pass mark:** 50% for every exam. No negative marking.
- **Applied Knowledge** exams are on-demand — book any time at a CBE centre.
- **Applied Skills** sit in four windows a year; max 4 papers per sitting.
- **Exemptions:** a relevant degree may exempt you from some Applied Knowledge papers — check ACCA's exemption calculator.

**How to start (do this during probation):**
1. Go to **accaglobal.com** → create a **myACCA** account and register as a student.
2. Use the **exemption calculator** to see which papers you can skip.
3. Pick your first exam (most start with **FA** or **MA** — on-demand, so no waiting).
4. Book through myACCA. Study with OpenTuition (free) + the files in this system.

**Cost:** there's an initial registration fee, an annual subscription, and per-exam fees
(Applied Knowledge papers are the cheapest; Applied Skills cost more). Confirm current
fees on accaglobal.com — they vary by region.

> **💡 Ask HLB about sponsorship.** Many audit firms pay or reimburse ACCA registration,
> exam fees, study materials, and give paid study leave for staff who pass probation.
> This is a major benefit and a strong reason to perform well now. Ask HR/your manager:
> *"Does the firm support ACCA study, and what are the conditions?"*

**Free study resources:**
- **OpenTuition** (opentuition.com) — free lectures, notes, mock exams for every paper. The best free ACCA resource.
- **ACCA Global Study Hub** — official syllabus, specimen exams, examiner reports, technical articles.
- **ACCA-X on edX** — free official introductory courses.
- Approved paid providers (more support, not required): **BPP**, **Kaplan**, **LSBF**, **First Intuition**.

## 2. Microsoft Office Specialist: Excel (your quick win) ⭐

**What it is:** Microsoft's official certification proving Excel skills. Recognised
worldwide, exam-based, and achievable within your probation with focused study.

**The exams (Microsoft 365 Apps versions):**

| Credential | Exam | Level | Covers |
|------------|------|-------|--------|
| **MOS: Excel Associate** | **MO-210** | Core | Worksheets, data, formulas, basic functions, charts, tables |
| **MOS: Excel Expert** | **MO-211** | Advanced | Advanced formulas, macros, advanced charts/tables, workbook management |

> Note: the older **Office 2016** MOS Excel exams (77-727 / 77-728) **retire on 30 June
> 2026** and are replaced by the **Microsoft 365 Apps** versions (MO-210 / MO-211). Book
> the Microsoft 365 Apps version.
> Source: [Microsoft Learn — MOS certifications](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/).

**Recommended path for you:** start with **MO-210 (Associate)** — it directly matches the
skills in `Tools/Excel-for-Auditors.md` and is realistic to pass during probation. Aim for
MO-211 (Expert) later once you're comfortable.

**How it works:** exams are taken at a **Certiport-authorised testing centre** (or online
proctored). Performance-based — you complete real tasks in Excel, not just multiple choice.

**Cost:** roughly **USD 100–150** per exam (varies by country/centre). Confirm with a local
Certiport centre. Some centres bundle a practice test (GMetrix).

**How to prepare:**
- **Microsoft Learn** (learn.microsoft.com) — free official study paths mapped to the exam.
- **GMetrix** — official-style practice tests (often bundled with exam vouchers).
- Work through `Tools/Excel-for-Auditors.md` and the drills in `Practice-Exercises.md`.
- **Coursera / DataCamp** — structured Excel courses (some free to audit).

## 5. Other professional credentials (for later in your career)

- **CIA** (Certified Internal Auditor — IIA) — the global internal-audit credential.
- **CISA** (Certified Information Systems Auditor — ISACA) — IT/systems audit.
- **CPA / ACA** — alternative chartered accountant routes.
- **CFA** — investment/finance focused (less relevant to external audit).
- **Diploma in IFRS (ACCA)** — focused IFRS credential, useful for reporting work.

Focus on ACCA + MOS Excel first. These are for the years ahead.

## 6. Your recommended certification timeline

| When | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Week 1–2** | Register a free Microsoft Learn account; start the MO-210 Excel study path. |
| **Week 2** | Create a myACCA account; run the exemption calculator; ask HLB about sponsorship. |
| **Week 4–6** | Take Coursera "Excel Skills for Business" (audit it free) alongside practice. |
| **Week 6–8** | Book the **MOS Excel Associate (MO-210)** exam at a local Certiport centre. |
| **Week 8 / post-probation** | Book your first **ACCA exam (FA or MA)** — on-demand. |
| **Months ahead** | Work through Applied Skills (PM, FR, FM, AA) + MOS Excel Expert / PL-300. |
